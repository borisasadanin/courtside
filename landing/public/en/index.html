    Your videos, your rules

    Your club owns its games

    Courtside runs in your club's own Open Source Cloud account. No other platform holds your videos, shows ads around your kids or decides what happens to the footage. Not even us: Eyevinn has no access.

    Your storage, your call

    Recordings live in your club's own storage. You decide how long to keep them, and you delete a game when a parent asks.

    Frequently asked questions

    What equipment do we need?

    An iPhone, a tripod and a connection through the venue's Wi-Fi or mobile data. A good phone mount and a power bank help on a long game day, but that's all you need.

    Where do we get the camera app?

    The camera app is free for iPhone and coming to the App Store. Want to try it before then? Get in touch. You pair the app with your Courtside by scanning the QR code in the admin view.

    Does it work with Android?

    For viewers, yes: the stream plays in any browser. The camera app is iPhone only for now.

    How soon can you watch a highlight?

    Right away. When the person filming taps the star, the play is clipped and ready to watch a few seconds later, while the game is still on. During breaks, all highlights play in a carousel.

    Can we share clips?

    Yes. Clips can be downloaded and shared. A shared clip link works without the PIN, so think about what you share and with whom.

    What does it actually cost?

    Courtside runs on Open Source Cloud. The Personal plan, €15 a month excl. VAT, covers about 10 games a month. Professional, €69 a month, has room for hundreds of games plus your own domain and email on your club's domain. No fees per team or per viewer.

    What are tokens?

    Tokens are Open Source Cloud's currency. Your plan tops up with a fixed amount every day. Courtside uses a little all the time and more only while you stream. The calculator above shows what your setup needs.

    What if we run out of tokens mid-game?

    Your plan tops up every day and unused tokens are saved. If you're close to the limit before a tournament, buy a token pack in advance. It never expires.

    Can anyone watch our games?

    Only people with the link, and with a PIN switched on, only people who also have the code. We always recommend a PIN when kids and teenagers are playing.

    How do we handle GDPR and consent?

    Courtside runs in your club's own account, so your club controls the videos. Eyevinn has no access. Tell parents that games are streamed and recorded, decide how long to keep them, and delete a game in the admin view if someone asks.

    What if we forget the admin code?

    The owner signs in here with their OSC account and picks Forgot the admin code?. The viewer code and all recordings stay as they are.

    Are the videos stored safely?

    Videos are kept in your club's own storage until you delete them or close the OSC account. There's no automatic backup of the videos, so download the clips you want to keep forever. Read more about your videos.

    Do we need someone who knows IT?

    No. Whoever sets up Courtside signs in, chooses a name and clicks Create. All keys and connections are handled automatically. After that it's one button on the phone.

    Does it work for sports other than basketball?

    Streaming and recording work for any sport, but periods, breaks and clip length are tuned for basketball today. More sports are next.
