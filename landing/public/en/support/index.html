Support

Getting started

Sign in on the front page with an Open Source Cloud account, choose a name and click Create. Then open your new address with the setup code and choose an admin code and a viewer code.

Pairing the camera app

Open the admin view of your Courtside and choose Lägg till kamera (Add camera). Scan the code with the app. To disconnect the phone, tap the gear in the app and choose to disconnect.

Forgot the admin code

Sign in on the front page with your OSC account and choose Forgot the admin code? next to your Courtside. More about your videos and codes.

Something isn't working

First check that the camera has a connection and that the app shows green system status. If you're stuck, write to sales@eyevinn.se and tell us your Courtside's address.
