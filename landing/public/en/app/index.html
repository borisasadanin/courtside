The camera app

The camera app turns an iPhone into the camera for your live streams. It only works together with a Courtside, so start your Courtside first.

For now the app is a test version, installed through Apple's TestFlight app. On the phone that will film:

1. Install TestFlight

Get TestFlight from the App Store. It is free and made by Apple.

TestFlight on the App Store

2. Open the invitation

Tap the button on the phone and choose Accept in TestFlight.

Open the invitation in TestFlight

3. Install the app

Tap Install next to Sports Camera – Eyevinn in TestFlight. Leave automatic updates on, and the phone gets new versions by itself.

4. Pair the app with your Courtside

Open the admin view of your Courtside on a computer and choose Lägg till kamera (Add camera). Open the app and scan the code shown.

The app needs an iPhone with iOS 16 or later. Stuck? See support.
