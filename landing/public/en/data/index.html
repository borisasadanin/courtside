Your videos and clips — how never to lose them

Courtside runs in your club's own Open Source Cloud (OSC) account. Your games and clips are in your own storage, in your account. You own them, and only those you give a code can see them. That also means no one else can bring them back if they disappear.

What makes videos disappear?

ThisHappens to the videos
You delete a game in the admin viewThat game is gone for good
You remove your Courtside on this siteEverything is gone: games, clips and settings
Someone removes the storage in OSCEverything is gone
The OSC account is cancelled or stops being paidThe services are switched off, and the videos may go with them
You forget the admin codeNothing. You get back in, see below
Courtside restarts or is updatedNothing

How to protect yourselves

Keep the OSC account paid and in the club's hands. Put the account on an email address the club owns, not on one parent who may leave. Make sure at least two people can sign in.

Save what you want to keep. OSC backs up databases, but not the video storage. There is no automatic copy of your games. Download the clips you want to keep forever.

Only delete on purpose. Removing your Courtside requires typing its name. It cannot be undone, not even by Eyevinn.

Forgot the admin code?

You are never locked out. Sign in on the front page with the OSC account that started your Courtside and choose Forgot the admin code?. You get a new setup code and choose a new admin code. The viewer code and all recordings stay. If you forgot the OSC password, reset it at OSC.

What Eyevinn can and cannot do

Eyevinn does not watch, use or share your videos. Open Source Cloud, which Eyevinn runs, stores them for you, and Eyevinn is then your club's data processor under OSC's data processing agreement. Courtside acts on your account only when you are signed in here yourselves and ask it to, such as at installation or Forgot the admin code?.

We cannot restore anything that was deleted. There is no copy with us.

The club's responsibility

The club decides about the videos and is responsible for telling parents that games are streamed and recorded, having a viewer code, deciding how long recordings are kept, and deleting a game when someone asks. You do that in the admin view.
