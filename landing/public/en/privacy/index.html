Privacy policy

Covers the website courtside.apps.osaas.io and the Courtside Camera app. Last changed 30 October 2026.

Who is responsible for what

Eyevinn Technology AB (reg. no. 556919-9952, Vasagatan 52, 111 20 Stockholm, Sweden) is responsible for this website and for the camera app.

Each club is responsible for its own Courtside: the games, the clips and what can be seen there. The club says on its own site, under Om inspelningarna (About the recordings), who is responsible and whom to contact. Questions about a particular club's streams and recordings go to that club.

When you visit the website

The website uses no cookies while you just read, no analytics and no resources from other companies. The fonts are served by the website itself.

The server logs every request: your IP address, the page asked for, your browser and the time. We use the logs to run the website and to troubleshoot. Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the service running and secure. The logs are kept by Open Source Cloud under its operations and security practices. No fixed retention period is stated.

When you sign in to start a Courtside

You sign in at Open Source Cloud. We then get temporary authority over your OSC account, so that we can create, update or remove your club's Courtside when you ask us to. That authority lives only in the server's memory during the sign-in, and ends after 30 minutes without activity and after 4 hours at most. We store no passwords.

One cookie, courtside_session, holds the sign-in together. It is necessary for the service to work and is used for nothing else.

When you create, update, repair or remove a Courtside, the server logs the club's short name and the name of the OSC account, so that we can troubleshoot if something fails. Legal basis: carrying out what you ask for.

The camera app

The app collects no data for us. It uses the camera and microphone to stream and to scan the club's pairing code. Video, audio and game information go only to the Courtside you paired the app with yourself. Eyevinn receives none of it. The app has no ads and no tracking.

Where the data is

The website and every club's Courtside run on Open Source Cloud, operated by Eyevinn at Elastx in Stockholm. The data is not transferred to countries outside the EU/EEA. See Open Source Cloud's privacy policy and list of sub-processors.

Your rights

You have the right to know what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to have its processing restricted and to object to it. Write to us and we answer within a month. If you are not satisfied you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, imy.se.
