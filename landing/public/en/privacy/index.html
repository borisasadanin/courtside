Privacy policy

Applies to the Courtside website courtside.apps.osaas.io and the camera app Courtside Camera.

The camera app

The app collects no data for us. It uses the camera and microphone to stream and to scan the club's pairing code. Video, sound and game information are sent only to the Courtside platform you paired the app with. Eyevinn receives none of it. The app has no ads and no tracking.

The club's Courtside

Each club runs its own Courtside in its own Open Source Cloud account. The club is responsible for the videos and for the information in it, such as team names and who streamed. Questions about a particular club's streams and recordings go to that club. The club can delete a game on request.

This website

When you sign in to start a Courtside, you sign in at Open Source Cloud. We keep the sign-in only for the session, to create the services in your account. We store no passwords and use no cookies for advertising or tracking.
